Common questions

Is the score an official HPAT score? No. It is an estimate on the published 2027 weighting using percentage correct; ACER's scaling is not public.

Why are mocks 1–6 locked? Their questions never appear in practice, the Daily 10 or review, so your mock estimate is not inflated by questions you have seen.

Does the timer run in the background? No. It counts only while Mindgrid is on screen, and your answers are saved instantly, so a mock resumes where you left it.
