Mindgrid privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

Mindgrid collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. It works offline. Your answers, mock results, test date and progress are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app or use Settings > Reset all progress.

If you tap “Report this item” or “Contact support”, your own mail app opens a draft addressed to the developer; nothing is sent unless you send it, and only what you write (plus the item ID) is included.

Mindgrid is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ACER. HPAT-Ireland is a trademark of ACER, used only to describe what the app prepares for.
